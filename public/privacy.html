Privacy Policy — Gittin

Updated 4 October 2026.

Gittin is an open-source Markdown and text editor that runs in your browser. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it never uploads your documents. This policy explains what it keeps on your device and when it contacts other services.

What stays on your device

Gittin keeps the following in your browser's storage:

  • your unsaved changes and recovery copies;
  • document titles;
  • the versions it compares against;
  • version history for files you open or save;
  • your preferences, toolbar choices and substitution rules;
  • access to the files and folders you open, so you can carry on where you left off.

It stays there until you or your browser removes it. Private browsing, your browser clearing storage to free space, or you clearing site data can remove it, so save or export anything you want to keep first.

Files you open are read and written on your device only, with the permissions your browser asks you to grant. Fonts are bundled with Gittin. Nobody who works on Gittin can see what stays on your device.

Offline copy

After Gittin has loaded once, your browser keeps a copy of Gittin's own files so it can start without a connection. This copy holds none of your documents. Gittin checks the website it came from for a new version, which you install by choosing Reload to update. Clearing site data removes the copy.

When Gittin contacts other services

Loading Gittin from a website sends requests to that website, which may log them as any website does. Images in your documents that are stored online load from their hosts, and following a link opens it. These services may receive your network or device details and have their own privacy policies. Gittin doesn't fetch link previews or check links in the background.

Opening and saving files, exporting and using the clipboard only happen when you choose, within your browser's controls. Spellcheck follows your browser and device settings and may use their services. Email this file and Email this draft show you the full text first, then open a draft in your mail app. Sending it is up to you, and your email provider's policy applies.

Managing your data

You can open and edit your files with other apps, export copies, and remove Gittin's data through your browser's site settings. This policy doesn't limit your legal rights. Changes to it are published on this page with a new date at the top.
